PrIAge logo, round 5: the AI carries the heartbeat

One idea, refined. The name already holds the story (Pr + IA + ge, with AI hidden as IA), so the mark is the name itself. The I and the A are set in blue, and the A's right leg carries on below the line as a single heartbeat before settling flat. Flat line: waiting. The beat: someone noticing. The A keeps its crossbar, so it still reads as PrIAge.

R1. The wordmark

The everyday logo: site header, documents, applications. The beat lives inside the name, so it never needs a separate icon next to it.

Small sizes (favicon, app icon, profile picture)

Where the name can't be read, the icon is the I and A alone; at 16 pixels, just the beating A. Still the name, not a generic heartbeat.
