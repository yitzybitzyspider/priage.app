PrIAge logo options

All three fix the "Prlage" misread by giving the capital I serifs, and set "IA" in seat blue so the name reads as Pr + IA + ge. None uses red: on the site, red only marks the patients a figure is about.

A. The seat

One waiting-room chair, the unit of every figure on the site, in a blue tile: tall back, seat, legs, so it reads as a chair even at 16 pixels. Simple, works as an app icon and favicon.

B. The row

Three joined chairs on a beam. The middle one is a paler blue: the person who is hard to see. Tells the problem in one glance, without saying how you'll solve it.

C. Wordmark only

No symbol: the name does the work. Serifed capital I, "IA" in seat blue, set like hospital wayfinding. Quietest and most flexible; weakest as an app icon.
