PrIAge logo options, round 3

Flat marks instead of app icons, each using medical language for "information about the person waiting". Descriptor: "Better information for the people making the call". No red, no hint of how it works.

G. The pulse under the chair

A waiting-room chair in profile. Its floor line turns into a single pulse beat and runs under the name: the condition of the person waiting, carried to whoever makes the call. Reads as a signature, not an icon.

K. The chart chair

A waiting-room chair whose back is a patient chart, clip and all. The chart is how medicine writes down what it knows about someone; here it travels with the person in the seat.

H. The plus seat

A medical plus with a waiting-room chair cut into its center: care, and the person waiting for it. The most "medical" at a glance; the least specific about information.
