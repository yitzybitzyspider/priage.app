One continuous line each: a heartbeat that flows into the waiting-room chair, so the medical signal and the person waiting are a single gesture. Descriptor: "Better information for the people making the call".

M. The beat that sits down

The line runs flat, beats once, and the spike's fall becomes the back of a chair; the line rests as the seat, steps down a leg, and carries on under the name. One stroke: the patient's condition and the patient waiting.

N. The beat, alone

Just the heartbeat line running under the name: the simplest medical signal for "how someone is doing". Cleanest; the waiting room lives in the site, not the mark.

O. The row that beats

One line draws a row of three seat backs, and the middle one rises as a heartbeat: the one person in the room whose condition matters right now. Waiting room and signal, still a single stroke.
