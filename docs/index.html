The waiting room is a blind spot.

Triage is a snapshot: a few questions and a set of vitals at the door. It sorts patients once. After that they wait, sometimes for hours, and within their triage level the line mostly runs on arrival order.

If someone gets worse in the chairs, the line doesn't know. Unless someone happens to notice, nothing changes until they collapse or their name is called.

Nobody should get sicker in a waiting room without someone noticing.

PrIAge is early, and working on it. If you work in an emergency department, or have waited in one, we'd like to hear from you.
