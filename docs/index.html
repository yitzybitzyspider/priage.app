After triage, the ER waiting room is a blind spot.

In 2022, US emergency departments handled 155 million visits.[2] Most begin with triage, a quick check at the door. Then patients wait, sometimes for hours, and it is hard for anyone to see who is getting worse.

PrIAge is working on ways to give ER nurses better information about patients while they wait. Nurses make the call. Right now we're talking with the people who do this work.

Some people give up and leave before being seen.

When waits stretch, some patients walk out before a doctor sees them. In Ontario, Canada, what happened to them afterward has gotten worse.[5]

Admitted, and still waiting for a bed.

Patients who need a hospital bed often keep waiting in the emergency department. In England, after five hours, deaths within 30 days rose steadily with every extra hour of waiting, up to twelve.[1]

Across England, it adds up.

England's emergency physicians estimated how many deaths were associated with the longest emergency department waits in 2023.[4]
