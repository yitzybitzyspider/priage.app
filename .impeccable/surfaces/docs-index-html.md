# Surface: priage.app landing page v2 (docs/index.html)

Mode: Persuade. Visitors: ER nurses, ER doctors, hospital leaders, patients and families who have waited in an ER, plus classmates and judges. Success: they grasp how big and how severe the waiting-room problem is from sourced numbers, and they email hello@priage.app to set up a conversation (the big ask).

Constraints: the problem's story only, no solution or mechanism; never imply reordering or skipping patients; never blame nurses or hospitals; not sensational, not playful; every number sourced and quoted as published; team public, photos black and white; US visitors only, no indexing or scraping.

Unresolved: a booking link may replace the email later.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Statistics told in seats. The ER waiting room's joined rows of molded chairs are the unit of every figure; one chair is one patient. Refuses the cause-page counter row and the health-tech split hero.

OWN-WORLD: Cool linoleum grey ground, hospital-blue molded seats on steel beams, fluorescent white panels, near-black ink, and one emergency-signage red reserved for the seats a figure is about. Overpass (wayfinding-sign lineage) for headings and numbers, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for reading. Stark geometric seat glyphs, never soft or cute.

STORY: After triage, the ER waiting room is a blind spot. The page follows a visit: the wait to be triaged (hero), triage misses, leaving unseen, admitted and still waiting for a bed, and the national toll in England, each in seats with its source sentence beside it. PrIAge states its goal (better information for ER nurses, nurses decide) and its stage (talking with the people who do this work); the ask is a one-tap email per role with promises, a copy button and a privacy note. It also serves applications and competitions.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sticky signage strip; left-aligned headline "The ER waiting room is a blind spot.", lede with the 155 million scale, the often-unmeasured wait before triage and the wait after it, a goal and stage line, "Talk with us"; then the pre-triage field of 100 chairs with 21 red on pale plates (about 1 in 5 of the sickest walk-ins waited over 15 minutes to be triaged). On phones the number and legend come before the field.

FORM: The Waiting Room, my top-ranked grounded candidate (rank 1, chosen as pick over assigned rank 4); seed b9fa80e1. Signature interaction: seats are visible by default; when a field comes into view its marked seats switch to red one after another in hard two-frame steps; reduced motion shows the final state. Scale (155 million) is a number beat, not a seat field, so one chair always means one patient.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
